What's inside

A scanner for every stage of recon. One place to read the results.

Rudo.ai doesn't replace these tools — it gives them root, in order, on one target, and brings every result back to a single prioritized list instead of ten scattered terminals.

Central Orchestrator

Define a target once. Rudo.ai sequences reconnaissance, enumeration, vulnerability discovery and reporting across all ten integrated tools automatically.

Universal AI Adapter

Run analysis on Groq's cloud, bring your own OpenRouter key, or go fully offline with a local Ollama model. Your call, per scan.

Ask Rudo.ai

A chat assistant trained on your scan's own results — ask "what should I fix first?" and get an answer grounded in the actual findings, not a generic checklist.

Attack Chain Analysis

Individually low-risk findings are correlated to surface the combinations that actually matter — not just a flat list of CVEs sorted by CVSS.

Scan-Diff

Re-scan the same target later and Rudo.ai shows exactly what changed: fixed, still open, or newly introduced since the last run.

Self-Hosted Deployment

Docker Compose, your own server. Scan data never has to leave your infrastructure unless you explicitly opt into a cloud AI provider.

How it works

From target to report in five coordinated stages

Target & scope definition

Point Rudo.ai at an asset you're authorized to test. Scope rules and SSRF protection keep scans off internal and localhost targets by default.

Information gathering

Subfinder, Httpx and Katana map subdomains, live hosts, and crawlable paths before anything destructive runs.

Vulnerability discovery

Nuclei, SQLMap, FFUF, Dalfox and Arjun run in parallel against the mapped surface.

AI-assisted analysis

Findings are prioritized by real-world severity and context, with related results linked into potential attack chains.

Reporting

A structured PDF is generated for the client or the system owner — and Scan-Diff keeps it comparable against the next run.

Data handling

Built to keep sensitive infrastructure details off third-party servers

None of this is a privacy guarantee on its own — but it gives you real control over what leaves your network.

Data Masking

IP addresses and email addresses are stripped before anything is sent to a cloud AI provider.

SSRF Protection

Internal targets and localhost addresses are rejected by default, reducing the risk of the platform being turned against your own network.

Self-Hosted by Default

Run entirely on your own server via Docker Compose — no multi-tenant SaaS, no shared infrastructure.
