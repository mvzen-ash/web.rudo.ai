What happens after checkout

Pay → confirm → download + key, in one flow

Choose a plan and pay

Checkout runs through our payment provider. You'll need a valid email — that's where everything else gets sent.

Payment confirmed

Once the payment clears, a signed, one-time activation key is generated and tied to your order.

Download starts

The installer for your platform begins downloading automatically from the confirmation page.

Key delivered as a .txt file

A plain-text file containing your one-time activation key is included with the download and also emailed as a backup.

Activate on first launch

Paste the key into Rudo.ai the first time you open it. Activation is verified online once, then the app runs normally from that machine.
